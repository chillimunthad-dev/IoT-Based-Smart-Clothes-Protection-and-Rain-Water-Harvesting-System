#IoT-Based Smart Clothes Protection and Rainwater Harvesting System

##1. Project Overview

The IoT-Based Smart Clothes Protection and Rainwater Harvesting System is designed to protect clothes from unexpected rainfall and collect rainwater for future use. This system uses an ESP32 microcontroller, rain sensor, servo motor, water level sensor, and buzzer to perform automatic operations.

When rain is detected, the servo motor automatically closes a protective cover over the drying clothes. At the same time, rainwater is collected and stored in a storage tank. When the tank reaches its full level, a buzzer alerts the user.

##2. Objectives

- To automatically protect drying clothes from unexpected rainfall.
- To collect and store rainwater for future use.
- To monitor the water level in the storage tank.
- To alert users when the tank is full.
- To reduce manual effort through IoT-based automation.

##4. Working Principle

1. The rain sensor continuously monitors rainfall.
2. When rain is detected, the sensor sends a signal to the ESP32.
3. The ESP32 processes the signal and controls the servo motor.
4. The servo motor closes the protective cover over the clothes.
5. Rainwater is collected and stored in a storage tank.
6. The water level sensor monitors the amount of water in the tank.
7. When the tank reaches the full level, the ESP32 activates the buzzer.
8. The buzzer alerts the user that the tank is full.
